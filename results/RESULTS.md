## Extra experiment 1 — is the random split honest? (leakage check)

Same tuned random forest, 5-fold cross-validation, two ways of making the folds:

| Metric | Random folds | Folds grouped by calendar month |
|---|---|---|
| AUROC | 0.83 | **0.58** |
| Kappa | 0.46 | **0.03** |
| R² (time to eruption) | 0.39 | **−0.17** |

Quakes in the same swarm have near-identical features and labels; with random folds they land in both train and test. Keeping each month together removes that.
